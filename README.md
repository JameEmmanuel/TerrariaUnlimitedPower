# UnlimitedPower

A tModLoader mod for Terraria that adds an intentionally absurdly overpowered accessory.

## Unlimited Power

The **Unlimited Power** accessory provides:

### Combat & Survival

- 9999 defense
- +9999% damage
- Infinite health
- Infinite mana
- Immunity to knockback
- Immunity to fall damage
- Maximum luck

### Movement & Exploration

- Infinite flight
- Extremely fast movement
- Extremely fast mining
- Infinite breath
- Immunity to fire blocks
- Immunity to lava
- Night vision
- Character emits light

### Fishing

- 9999 fishing power

The accessory is intentionally ridiculous and is designed primarily as a fun/experimental mod.

## Recipes

The Unlimited Power accessory can be crafted using either of the following recipes.

### Standard

- 10 Wood
- 10 Dirt Block

### Ash Variant

- 10 Ash Wood
- 10 Ash Block

Neither recipe requires a crafting station.

## Status

**Beta**

This is an experimental mod and features may change in future versions.

- Terraria
