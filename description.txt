# UnlimitedPower

Ever wished Terraria had a completely balanced, carefully designed accessory?

Yeah, me neither.

**UnlimitedPower** adds one ridiculously overpowered accessory to Terraria. Crafted from nothing more than **10 Wood and 10 Dirt**, it gives you powers that completely ignore the concept of game balance.

## What does it do?

**Combat & Survival**

* +9999 Defense
* Infinite Health
* Infinite Mana
* Maximum Luck
* No Knockback
* No Fall Damage

**Movement & Exploration**

* Infinite Flight
* Extremely Fast Movement
* Extremely Fast Mining
* Infinite Breath
* Fire Block Immunity
* Lava Immunity
* Night Vision
* Emits Light

**Fishing**

* 9999 Fishing Power

## Crafting

### Standard Recipe

* 10 Wood
* 10 Dirt Block

### Ash Recipe

* 10 Ash Wood
* 10 Ash Block

No crafting station required.

## Why?

Because Terraria is obviously too difficult when you're not able to mine a block in approximately 0.00001 seconds while flying indefinitely through lava.

This mod is intended as a **fun, absurd, and experimental power fantasy**, not as a balanced gameplay experience.

**Current status: Beta**
